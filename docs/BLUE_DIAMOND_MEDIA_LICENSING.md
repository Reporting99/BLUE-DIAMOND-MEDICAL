# Media Licensing Register

**Licensing is tracked independently of content review and independently of approval state.** An asset can be content-safe, correctly matched, approved and rendering, and still have unresolved licensing. Three of the four currently-approved assets are in exactly that position.

The rule this register exists to enforce: **“it was on the client’s old website” is not “licence verified for reuse.”** All 37 imported assets were scraped from `bluediamondmedical.ca` / `bluediamondmedicalaesthetics.ca` by the importer. That establishes provenance, not rights.

## Summary

| Classification | Count | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `VERIFIED_CLIENT_OWNED` | 5 | Clinic’s own mark or own staff photography |
| `VERIFIED_LICENSED` | 0 | A licence has been produced and checked — **none yet** |
| `THIRD_PARTY_ATTRIBUTED` | 12 | Manufacturer/partner trademark, or another physician’s credit, visible in the asset |
| `LEGACY_PROVENANCE_ONLY` | 14 | Only evidence is “it was on the old site” |
| `UNKNOWN` | 6 | Patient imagery of unknown origin; no attribution, consent or licence |
| **Total** | **37** | |

**`VERIFIED_LICENSED` is zero.** No licence document has been produced for any of the 37.

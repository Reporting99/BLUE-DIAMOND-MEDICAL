# Blue Diamond Medical English house style

Editorial standard established 2026-09-08 for Calgary, Alberta. This is a language standard, not clinical approval. Business facts, clinical protocols, device identity and fees follow the authority hierarchy in the audit brief. Unresolved conflicts are recorded rather than guessed.

- Write for patients in Calgary. Use West Springs for the main clinic and retain the separate Citizen Studio location wherever an Elite iQ™ treatment is discussed. Do not infer which clinic owns or operates a device.
- Use **Blue Diamond Medical** as the display brand, **Blue Diamond Medical Clinic** for the clinic and **Blue Diamond Medical Aesthetics** for aesthetic services. Preserve registered names in structured data pending business confirmation.
- Canadian spelling: patient-centred, practising (verb), practice (noun), colour, labour, travelling, counselling, anaesthetic, well-being, microneedling, radiofrequency, post-inflammatory. Use **pediatric** consistently as an accepted Canadian spelling; retain official titles and quoted source titles.
- Use family physician/family doctor, prescription renewal, walk-in care and patient. Avoid American insurance assumptions and unsupported specialty titles. AHS is an organization; the provincial physician insurance plan is AHCIP.
- Retain official product names, including **Total Defense + Repair**. Canadian spelling does not rename products. Keep slugs and identifiers stable when changing display spelling.
- Use calm, direct CTAs: Book an Appointment, Request a Consultation, Call the Clinic, View Medical Services. FAQs answer the patient's question first.
- Use sentence case in prose and existing title case for short navigation labels. Do not capitalize generic medical disciplines within sentences. Use curly apostrophes where supported; straight apostrophes in existing code are acceptable when rendered consistently.
- Use en dashes for ranges: 20–30 minutes, 3–5 treatments, 4–6 weeks. Prefer a.m./p.m. for prose times. Preserve all actual time and fee values. Keep telephone display driven by the existing central configuration; do not hand-type alternative numbers.
- Explain terms once, then use the abbreviation. Avoid hype and unqualified guarantees. A qualified outcome is still a clinical claim and must retain its source and any unresolved review flag.
- Preserve clinical detail, contraindications, aftercare and durations. Do not silently remove source content because it is difficult to edit. Record source omissions and clinical holds individually.
